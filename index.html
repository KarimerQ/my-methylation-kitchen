Daily goals

Cup targets

  • Leafy greens: 2 cups
  • Cruciferous vegetables: 2 cups
  • Colorful vegetables: 3 cups
  • Beets: 1 to 2 medium
  • Seeds: 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds plus 1/4 cup sunflower seeds
  • Berries plus 2 servings of whole fruit

Why supplement

  • B12: a vegan diet has no reliable B12 source.
  • Vitamin D3: only if levels are low.
  • Algae EPA and DHA: the omega 3s without fish.
  • Magnesium at dinner: only if needed.

Why train

Resistance training keeps muscle and supports metabolic health.

Why meditate

Meditation brings stress down and helps you sleep better.
