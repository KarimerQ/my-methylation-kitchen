Why supplement

  • B12: a vegan diet has no reliable B12 source.
  • Vitamin D3: only if levels are low.
  • Algae EPA and DHA: the omega-3s without fish.
  • Magnesium at dinner: only if needed.

Resistance training keeps muscle and supports metabolic health.

The Seven Tenets of Aging

Why cells age, in seven categories, and what your plan does about each.

Folate Sulforaphane Nrf2 Microbiome Omega-3 Proteostasis Telomeres Epigenetic age

With your plan: methyl donors in, waste cleared, energy steady.

Evidence: Horvath 2013, Genome Biology: DNA methylation patterns predict age. Fitzgerald et al. 2021, Aging: a diet and lifestyle program was linked to younger methylation age.

In your plan: Methyl donors: folate from greens, legumes, beets.

Evidence: Lopez-Otin et al. 2023, Cell: mitochondrial dysfunction is one of the hallmarks of aging.

In your plan: Training and sleep support mitochondria; algae omega-3 covers the EPA and DHA fats.

Evidence: Harrison et al. 2009, Nature: rapamycin, an mTOR inhibitor, extended lifespan in mice.

In your plan: The 11 AM to 7 PM eating window touches AMPK and mTOR signaling.

Evidence: Lopez-Otin et al. 2023, Cell: loss of proteostasis and disabled autophagy are hallmarks of aging.

In your plan: The daily overnight fast gives autophagy its window.

Evidence: Franceschi et al. 2000, Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences: inflammaging, chronic low-grade inflammation that rises with age.

In your plan: Sulforaphane signals Nrf2; omega-3s support inflammation balance.

Evidence: Lopez-Otin et al. 2023, Cell: cellular senescence and stem cell exhaustion are hallmarks of aging.

In your plan: A protein source at every meal feeds different cell needs.

Evidence: Uribarri et al. 2010, Journal of the American Dietetic Association: dietary advanced glycation end products and their health effects.

In your plan: Plant meals cooked gently make fewer AGEs than charred animal fats.
